Manual acceptance:
- Upload alongside the main mod, configure from ESM only, apply and restart.
- All false reproduces the main mod's existing behavior.
- Each unique candidate and combinations (including all true): ground hit, target hit, disappearance after configured
  lifetime, shooter logout, repeated shots, and an unmodified second client.
- Test at least two different burn_time_ms values on a looping ground candidate.
- Confirm no native explosion/terrain damage or native extra DoT from previews.
- Compare ordinary enemy attacks/throwables with the test disabled/enabled.
- Record invisible/short-lived attached and one-shot candidates as such; do not
  mistake a configured entity lifetime for a verified continuous particle loop.
- Disable the companion, apply and restart; original config and behavior return.
